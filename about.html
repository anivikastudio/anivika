WHO WE ARE

Home of Original Worlds.

Anivika is an independent creative studio focused on building original characters, stories, and universes.

Founded in 2021, the studio explores animation, illustration, design, and visual storytelling as tools for creating memorable worlds and experiences.

While technologies continue to evolve, the mission remains unchanged: creating original worlds worth exploring.

THE JOURNEY

Every World Begins Somewhere.

2021

The Beginning

Anivika began with a simple ambition: to create original worlds, characters, and stories.

What started as an independent creative effort became the first step toward building original universes and long-term intellectual properties.

2024

The Foundation

As the studio continued to grow, the vision became clearer and the foundation became stronger.

New projects, improved workflows, and a growing focus on original storytelling helped shape the direction of the studio.

2026+

The Worlds Ahead

Today, Anivika continues developing original stories, characters, and universes through Anivika Originals.

The journey is still unfolding, with new worlds and future generations of characters waiting to be discovered.
